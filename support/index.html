01Getting started

How do I add a friend?

Tap + on the Clocks tab. Type their name and pick their city, or tap From contacts to choose someone from your phone. Overlap guesses their country from the phone number; you can change it.

How does Overlap know if someone is awake?

Each friend has awake hours, 7am to 11pm unless you change them. Open the friend and set Usually awake to when they wake up and go to sleep, in their local time. The ring around their avatar and the pill next to their time follow those hours.

How do groups work?

Open a friend and pick their groups under Groups. The chips at the top of the Clocks tab then filter your list, the globe and the planner. Rename groups in Settings › Groups.

02Planning calls

How is the best time to call worked out?

On the Plan a call tab, Overlap looks at everyone in the group you pick and finds the hours when the most of them are awake. Public holidays in their countries are shown right where you plan.

A friend's time looks wrong.

Check the city on their page: Overlap uses that city's time zone, including daylight saving. If they've moved, pick their new city. Turn on Settings › Clock change warnings to hear a day before their clocks change.

03Notifications

How do I get told when a friend wakes up?

Open the friend and turn on Tell me when they wake up. It only goes off while you're awake, so it never wakes you.

My reminders don't go off.

Check that notifications are allowed for Overlap in your phone's settings. On some Android phones, battery saving can delay alarms: set Overlap's battery use to Unrestricted.

04App and data

Can I use 24-hour time or dark mode?

Yes. In Settings › Appearance, pick System, Light or Dark, an accent colour, and 12-hour, 24-hour or your device clock.

Is the home-screen widget on iPhone?

Not yet. The widget is on Android for now: Settings › Add widget to home screen.

How do I move my friends to a new phone?

Your friends are stored only on your phone, so they don't move automatically. You'll need to add them again on the new phone.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings › Remove all friends, or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
